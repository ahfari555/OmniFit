Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At OmniFit, accessible from omni-fit.vercel.app, your privacy is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by OmniFit and how we use it.

Client-Side Processing

All calculations (TDEE, Macros, Body Fat, BMI, BMR, etc.) are executed entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not store, log, or transmit your personal health metrics, body weights, or medical timelines to any external database.

Log Files & Analytics

Like many standard websites, OmniFit uses hosting providers and analytics tools (such as Vercel Analytics and Google AdSense) that may collect standard non-personal visitor data including IP addresses, browser types, and referring pages to optimize user experience.
